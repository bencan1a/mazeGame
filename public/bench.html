Canvas bench — buffer ceiling and blit rate

No generator, no renderer, no framework. Measures whether one big offscreen buffer can be allocated, and how fast it blits under pan and zoom.

Idle.

Device

1. Buffer ceiling

Allocates square buffers of increasing size, draws into each, and reads a pixel back. Safari does not throw when it runs out — it hands back a blank canvas — so the readback is what detects the ceiling.

2. Blit rate

Draws 400 polylines into the buffer once, then blits a moving, zooming sub-rectangle to the screen for 4 seconds per size. This is the frame budget the two-layer renderer would live in.
